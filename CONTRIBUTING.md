# Contributing

This is a local example. Keep it that way.

- Do not add real names, email addresses, phone numbers, customer orders, or
  photos of real goods.
- Do not add a public host, a deploy workflow, or an account id.
- `.env` stays on your machine. Copy `.env.example` and leave the local
  defaults unless you are pointing at your own local Postgres.
- Sample people in tests and in the seed are roles (`Merch One`, `Cutter One`),
  on `example.com` or `factory.example`.

Run `pnpm test` before sending a change. The license is BSD-2-Clause.
